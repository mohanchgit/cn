Store

Intermediate nodes store the entire message.

Forward

After storing the message, the node forwards it to the next destination.

Advantage

No dedicated path is required.

Limitation: Large messages require significant storage and may introduce considerable delay.

NRZ-L Example

NRZ-L

The signal level represents the binary value.

Advantage

Simple implementation and efficient use of bandwidth.

Limitation

Long sequences of identical bits can cause synchronization problems.

Manchester Encoding
Manchester encoding uses a transition in the middle of every bit period.
1
0
1
1
Manchester Signal

Transition

Every bit contains a transition at the center of the bit period.

Synchronization

Frequent transitions help the receiver maintain clock synchronization.

Trade-off

Requires more bandwidth than simple NRZ encoding.

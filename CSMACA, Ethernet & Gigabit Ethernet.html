📡 1. CSMA/CA – Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance

CSMA/CA is a medium access control technique primarily associated with wireless LANs such as IEEE 802.11 Wi-Fi.

Its main goal is to reduce the probability of collisions when multiple wireless devices want to transmit at the same time.

Simple Idea:

Listen → Wait if Busy → Random Backoff → Transmit

📚 Important CSMA/CA Concepts

1 Carrier Sense

A wireless station first checks whether the communication channel is currently busy.

2 Random Backoff

If the channel is busy, the station waits for a randomly selected backoff period.

3 ACK

The receiver sends an acknowledgment frame after successfully receiving the frame.

4 Collision Avoidance

CSMA/CA attempts to reduce the chance of collisions before transmission occurs.

5 RTS / CTS

Request-to-Send and Clear-to-Send mechanisms can help reduce the hidden-node problem in wireless networks.

⚖️ CSMA/CA vs CSMA/CD

Feature CSMA/CA CSMA/CD
Full Form Collision Avoidance Collision Detection
Common Association Wireless LAN / Wi-Fi Traditional shared Ethernet
Main Approach Try to avoid collisions Detect collisions after transmission starts
ACK Commonly used Not the same mechanism

🔌 2. Ethernet

Ethernet is a widely used LAN technology standardized by IEEE 802.3.

Ethernet defines how devices communicate over wired local-area networks, including frame formats and physical transmission technologies.

Common Ethernet Media:

Copper cables, fiber-optic cables and related physical-layer technologies.

⚡ 3. Gigabit Ethernet

Gigabit Ethernet refers to Ethernet technologies capable of transmitting data at a nominal rate of 1 gigabit per second (1 Gb/s).

Gigabit Ethernet is commonly associated with IEEE 802.3 specifications and can operate over copper or fiber, depending on the implementation.

🔧 Gigabit Ethernet Examples

1 1000BASE-T

Gigabit Ethernet over twisted-pair copper.

Commonly uses Category 5e or better twisted-pair cabling for suitable installations.

2 1000BASE-SX

Gigabit Ethernet over multimode fiber, commonly used for shorter-distance links.

3 1000BASE-LX

Gigabit Ethernet using optical fiber, suitable for longer-distance network links than many short-range fiber implementations.

📝 Key Learning Points

1
CSMA/CA is primarily associated with wireless communication and attempts to avoid collisions.
2
Ethernet is a major wired LAN technology standardized under IEEE 802.3.
3
Gigabit Ethernet provides a nominal data rate of 1 Gb/s.
4
Modern switched full-duplex Ethernet networks generally do not use CSMA/CD for ordinary point-to-point links.
